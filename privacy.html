Privacy & terms

Last updated: September 2026

1. Privacy policy — launcher

DontCam Client sends none of your data to our servers — because we don't have any. Accounts, settings and profiles are stored only locally on your computer (accounts.json, settings.json, profiles/).

Microsoft login uses the official OAuth2 flow (system browser → Xbox Live → XSTS → Minecraft Services). Tokens stay on your device and are used only to sign you into the game. We don't sell, share or analyze any data.

2. Privacy policy — website

This website uses no cookies and no trackers. Your browser's localStorage holds only your language and theme choice. We collect no e-mails and no visit statistics.

3. Terms of use

The launcher is free and provided 'as is', without warranty. You use it at your own risk. Playing on premium servers requires a legally purchased Minecraft account.

Don't modify the launcher to bypass protections and don't use it to break server rules — consequences (e.g. bans) are yours alone.
